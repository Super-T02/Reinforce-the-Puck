\subsection{Training on Simple Environments} \label{subsec:simple_eval}

We trained the introduced algorithms first on simple environments, also used in the lecture. Therefore, we compare \ac{TD3} with the different buffers and basic \ac{SAC} on Pendulum, Lunar Lander and HalfCheetah. In this step we exclude our hybrid approach because it was specifically developed for the hockey environment. With the attached hyperparameters (Appendix \ref{sec:hyper_simple}), we can observe that \ac{SAC} has higher average returns after 10000 steps than all \ac{TD3} implementations and \ac{TD3} with \ac{BER} outperforms the other buffer implementations in HalfCheetah and Lunar Lander (\autoref{tab:avg_reward}). Additionally, \ac{PER} performs always better than \ac{ER}. Although, \ac{BPER} is converging faster and hits earlier better rewards (\autoref{sec:simple_plots}) with an faster converging policy, it has the worst final return.
