\subsection{Hockey Environment} \label{subsec:hockey_eval}

The performance on the hockey environment is evaluated with the average final performance (\autoref{tab:avg_reward}) and the corresponding evaluation reward of the training in 600000 environment steps (\autoref{fig:eval-own-reward}). Therefore, we train \ac{SAC} and \ac{TD3} on the original environment reward and our own reward. The opponents are the strong/weak basic opponents, \ac{SAC} foundation, \ac{TD3} foundation and Hybrid foundation agents, which are pretrained on the strong/weak opponents. To stabalize training, the opponents change after 10000 environment steps (100 epochs). The evaluation reward contains only the win/lose signal with $r_{eval} \in \left\{-10, 0, 10\right\}$ and is recorded every 5000 steps (50 epochs) over 20 runs. The average reward is then saved and plotted in \autoref{fig:eval-own-reward}. All other statistics, like critic-, actor-loss and training reward provided in the appendix \autoref{sec:hockey-plots}. The final average evaluation reward over 100 runs is also provided in \autoref{tab:avg_reward}.
